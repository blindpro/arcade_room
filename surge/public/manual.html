Surge
an audio-first endless runner

Introduction

Surge is an audio-first endless runner on a 5-lane road. You always run forward; you steer between lanes, jump hazards, shoot enemies and collect coins, gems and cores. Everything you hear is spatialized around you: a sound panned left means the thing is in a lane to your left, and the louder it is the closer it is — so you can play the whole game by ear. Obstacles and enemies whisper as they approach, pickups ping at the pitch of their value, and your footsteps, weapon and the music all tell you how fast you are going. What you earn feeds a persistent profile of currencies, a shop, achievements and daily missions.

System Requirements

This is best experienced with headphones and a gamepad.

Real-time synthesis is an extremely CPU-intensive process. It will attempt to run on any machine that meets its minimum requirements; however, you may experience stuttering or drop-outs when running below the recommended requirements.

Minimum System Requirements

CPU SSE2 capable (64-bit)
RAM 2 GB

Recommended System Requirements

CPU 3.5 GHz Quad-Core (64-bit)
RAM 4 GB

Browser Support

The HTML5 build is intended to be played in a recent version of a Chromium-based browser, such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Opera. Browser extensions that alter page behavior are not recommended. Other browsers must support modern JavaScript features and the current Web Audio API specification.

How to Play

You run down a 5-lane road at a speed that climbs the further you go. Obstacles block the lanes ahead — some are jumped, some must be avoided by steering, and low walls need the EMP item. Enemies walk, fly or hover in your way and must be shot with your weapon. Each environment hands you the weapon suited to it: a pistol in the city, a rifle in the desert, a shotgun in the volcano, a bow on the tundra, a laser in space, a dart gun in the jungle, a harpoon in the ocean, a plasma rifle in neon and a shard launcher in the cavern. Fire at an enemy to build up charge and unleash a charged shot for big damage; hold to auto-fire with automatic weapons. Coins, gems and cores are yours to pick up, and the sewer side-areas hide bonus treasure behind a tunnel entrance. There are four modes. Practice lets you learn the streets with no stakes. Sprint is a 90-second race for the best distance. Adventure takes you through nine environments, one 400-metre level each, ending in a victory screen. Endless ramps through the environments forever, re-pitching the difficulty every segment. Between runs, spend your currencies in the shop: unlock the rocket launcher, speed items like overdrive and brake, and permanent passives like a starting shield, a magnet for pickups, double coins and an extra health bar. The Codex screen (from the main menu) catalogues every world, hazard, enemy, weapon and item. Achievements and daily missions award extra coins, gems, cores and tokens.
