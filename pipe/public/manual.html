Pipe
an audio-first tunnel run

Introduction

Pipe is an audio-first tunnel run. You fly down a metal pipe that grows wider with every level, and rings block your way — each ring has an opening you must be inside when you reach it, or you slam into its wall, lose a life and get thrown back to try again. The opening hums at its centre and its edges sweep when it first appears, so you can thread it entirely by ear. Every 10th level is a bonus cavern full of items pitched by their value. Speed rises every level and the game never ends: your score is how far you got plus the bonus points.

System Requirements

This is best experienced with headphones and a gamepad.

Real-time synthesis is an extremely CPU-intensive process. It will attempt to run on any machine that meets its minimum requirements; however, you may experience stuttering or drop-outs when running below the recommended requirements.

Minimum System Requirements

CPU SSE2 capable (64-bit)
RAM 2 GB

Recommended System Requirements

CPU 3.5 GHz Quad-Core (64-bit)
RAM 4 GB

Browser Support

The HTML5 build is intended to be played in a recent version of a Chromium-based browser, such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Opera. Browser extensions that alter page behavior are not recommended. Other browsers must support modern JavaScript features and the current Web Audio API specification.

How to Play

You fly down a metal pipe and your speed rises with every level. The pipe is divided into 1-meter lanes, and from time to time a ring blocks it: the ring has an opening that spans some of the lanes, and you must be inside that opening when you reach the ring. When a new ring becomes your target, its left and right edges sweep so you hear how wide the opening is, then its centre hums — steer left or right until the hum is dead ahead and you will thread it. Pass five openings and the level advances: the pipe widens and you fly faster. Every 10th level is a bonus cavern: no rings, just valuable items that whisper at the pitch of their points, then an opening that spans the whole pipe so you can't miss. Miss an opening at any other time and you slam into the wall, lose a life and get thrown back to re-approach it — you start with three lives. The game never ends; your score is how far you got plus the bonus points.
