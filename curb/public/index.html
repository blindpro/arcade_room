How to play

You are a hedgehog crossing a busy road by ear.

  • Press Up to step toward the far curb and Down to step back. Reaching a curb scores 100.
  • Each vehicle pans across the stereo field and grows louder as it nears the centre of the road. Cross while your lane is clear.
  • Press Space any time to hear your score.
  • Dawdling in the road costs points, so keep moving.
  • Press Escape to return to the menu.
