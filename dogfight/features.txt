Feature ideas to make Dogfight more interesting
===============================================

1. Pickups / Power-ups
   Scatter items around the arena that despawn and respawn:
   - Health repair kits
   - Ammo restock (missiles)
   - Temporary shield (absorbs one hit)
   - Speed boost pickup (shorter, stronger than the shift boost)
   - Proximity mines (lay behind you, detonate when an enemy passes near)

2. AI difficulty levels
   Let the player choose "Easy / Normal / Hard" before starting.
   Easy: AI turns slower, fires less often, worse aim.
   Hard: AI leads targets, dodges missiles, uses boost.

3. Different plane classes
   Let the player pick a plane before the round:
   - Interceptor: fast, frail, rapid-fire guns
   - Bomber: slow, tough, heavy guns, extra missiles
   - Scout: balanced speed, longer missile lock range

4. More game modes
   - Team deathmatch (2v2, 3v3)
   - Last stand (waves of AI, survive as long as possible)
   - Target practice (stationary or slow-moving drones, score chase)

5. Arena hazards
   - Moving obstacles that cross the arena
   - Turbulence zones that push planes off course
   - Dead zones that drain health over time

6. Combo / streak system
   Award bonus score for rapid consecutive kills or hits.
   Announce streaks ("Double kill!", "Triple kill!") to build tension.

7. Replay / best-round summary
   After each match, show the player's stats:
   - Kills, deaths, damage dealt/taken
   - Accuracy (shots fired vs hits)
   - Longest streak
   Compare against their personal bests.
