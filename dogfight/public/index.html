    How to play

    Controls

    • Up - throttle up
    • Down - air brake / throttle down
    • Left Right - bank and turn
    • Space - fire machine guns
    • F - fire missile when locked
    • Q - announce enemy planes and their bearing
    • F1 score, F2 planes left, F3 nearest target, F4 health
    • Escape - quit to menu
    • Enter / Space - confirm in menus

    How it sounds

    Each plane has a spatial engine voice. Planes in front sound brighter; planes behind sound darker.

    A missile lock tone tightens as an enemy enters your nose cone. Incoming missiles warn from their direction.

    Gunfire chips health, missiles hit hard, and ramming causes huge damage to both planes.

    Goal

    Be the last plane alive. Damage you deal scores points; eliminations and survival score bonuses.
